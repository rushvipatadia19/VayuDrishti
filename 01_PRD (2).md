## §1 Problem statement
- Problem statement (paste exactly): Develop an Environmental Intelligence & Compliance Management System that helps communities, industries and public authorities understand environmental incidents, recognize emerging risks and take informed action. The system should connect environmental observations, predictive insights, applicable standards and incident-management workflows to turn a reported concern into an evidence-backed assessment and a trackable response.
- Our restatement in 2 sentences: When someone reports a suspected air-pollution spike, our app shows the real readings, flags what is unusual, checks them against the applicable air-quality standard and suggests a next step. It then lets a responsible person log corrective actions and track the case from open to closed.
- Who experiences it: residents near industrial areas, factory/compliance managers, and local pollution-control officers.
- Source/theme: Infomatrix HackOps 2, Environmental Intelligence & Compliance Management System.

## §2 Problem analysis

| Dimension | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Root cause | Readings, complaints, standards and follow-up records live in different places, so nobody sees one evidence-backed picture of an incident |

## §3 Solution overview and USP
- One paragraph: "Our app turns a vague air-pollution complaint into an evidence-backed case in under a minute, with the readings, the exceeded limit, the cited standard and a next step, for residents and compliance officers."
- **USP (one sentence):** we are different because every flag shows its evidence (readings, threshold, cited clause) and the same screen carries the case through to closure.
- Is AI really needed here? Partly. Anomaly detection, short-term forecast and the threshold check are plain code (rolling z-score, trend, comparison with limits) so they are fast and explainable. A pretrained Hugging Face model (called through Inference Providers, no training) is used only to explain the applicable standard, using the standards snippet we pass in, and to suggest a next step in plain language. It never decides whether a limit is exceeded.

## §11 Glossary
- **PM2.5 / PM10:** particles under 2.5 / 10 micrometres, measured in µg/m³.
- **NAAQS:** National Ambient Air Quality Standards set by CPCB (India).
- **CPCB:** Central Pollution Control Board.
- **AQI:** Air Quality Index, a 0 to 500 score summarising pollutant levels.
- **Anomaly (z-score):** how many standard deviations a reading is from its recent average.
- **24-hour average:** the averaging period the standard limits use.
